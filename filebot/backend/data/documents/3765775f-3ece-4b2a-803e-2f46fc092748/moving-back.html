Moving back to Canada Canadians moving back to Canada after living abroad need to, among other things, find a job and a place to live, get a new driver’s licence and health card, find a doctor, register to vote in their new riding, and find childcare, schools and recreational facilities. All of these pressing issues fall under provincial or territorial responsibility, and each province and territory has its own requirements.
