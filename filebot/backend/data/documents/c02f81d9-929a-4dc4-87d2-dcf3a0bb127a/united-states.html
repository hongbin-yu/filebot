Emergency services

Dial 911 for emergency assistance.

Consular assistance

During business hours: contact Canadian Citizen Services at ccs.scc@international.gc.ca or 1-844-880-6519.

For emergency assistance at any time: contact the Emergency Watch and Response Centre in Ottawa. You can also call toll-free at 1-888-949-9993.
