Find a Service Canada office near you If you have general questions about the CDCP and how it works, you do not need to provide identification (ID). However, if you have specific questions about your file, you need to show ID and answer verification questions. Accepted forms of ID Some forms of ID will be accepted on their own, while other forms of ID require validation using a secondary piece.
