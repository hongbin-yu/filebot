You must meet all 4 of the eligibility requirements before applying. Apply online What you need to apply You’ll need to provide the following information for each applicant, and your spouse or common-law partner (if you have one): Social Insurance Number (SIN) (if available for children) date of birth full name home and mailing address list of dental coverage through government social programs (if applicable) you and your spouse or common-law partner (if applicable) must have filed your tax return in Canada for the previous year and have received your notice of assessment If you are ready to apply or if your previous coverage has expired Use My Service Canada Account (MSCA) Steps to apply: Sign in or create your My Service Canada Account (MSCA) using the first button below. The Canadian Dental Care Plan is shown at the top of the page on your MSCA dashboard. Click on "Apply for the Canadian Dental Care Plan" and follow the instructions. Apply via MSCA Digital communications Select "digital" as your communications preference to receive email notifications. You can also access letters from the CDCP and update your personal information through MSCA. By signing up, you'll get important updates in one place, and it is faster than waiting for letters in the mail. If you can't use MSCA Apply via canada.ca If you can't use MSCA or canada.ca Call Service Canada at 1-833-537-4342. For TTY, call 1-833-677-6262. Renewing your yearly coverage As a CDCP member, you need to renew your coverage every year during the renewal period to confirm that you still meet the eligibility requirements. If you do not renew between April 15 and June 1, 2026 for the upcoming 2026-2027 benefit year, your coverage will end on June 30, 2026. If you missed the June 1, 2026 deadline, you will need to submit a new application. Members who applied on or before April 14, 2026 still need to renew their coverage before June 1, 2026. Be advised that there will be a gap in your coverage and oral health care services received during a gap will not be covered nor reimbursed retroactively. You should always confirm that your CDCP coverage is active before receiving treatment from your oral health provider. Please note that your co-payment level could change when you renew. Any preauthorized treatments will be paid at the co-payment level at the time of treatment. If someone is helping you apply You can ask someone you trust or a delegate to help you apply for the CDCP. Applying with the help of someone you trust You can ask a trusted person to help you apply by phone. This could be a: friend relative caregiver translator, or interpreter You must be able to give clear consent that you agree to let them help you. Applying through a delegate A delegate is someone who has the legal authority to represent you and make decisions on your behalf. They can represent you if they’re listed as your delegate on documents such as: power of attorney mandate trusteeship Before you apply, your delegate must prove that they have the legal authority to represent you.
