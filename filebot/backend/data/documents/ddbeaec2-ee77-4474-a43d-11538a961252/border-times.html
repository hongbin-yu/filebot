Saint-Bernard-de-Lacolle under redevelopment: Expect border delays until Winter 2027

Work to redevelop this border crossing's primary inspection line is underway and expected to end in Winter 2027. As a result, wait times may be higher than normal. We strongly encourage you to monitor traffic and adjust your route accordingly.
