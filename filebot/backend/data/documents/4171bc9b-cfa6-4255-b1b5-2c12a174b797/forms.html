It is important to first determine which government institution is most likely to have the information you are seeking. Refer to Information About Programs and Information Holdings for a description of personal information held by the institution or Contact the Access to Information and Privacy (ATIP) Coordinator of the institution. Make an online request Use the ATIP Online Request Portal, for a faster, easier and more convenient way to submit access to information or privacy requests. Apply online today to certain government institutions to save time and postage. Mail a request If you are unable to use the ATIP Online Request Portal, you may request information using the Access to Information Request Form (TBC/CTC 350-57) (available in PDF or HTML formats), or send a letter clearly explaining what records you are seeking, to the ATIP Coordinator of the government institution holding the information. Report on the Access to Information Act (TBS/SCT 350-62) Title Form for the Statistical Report on the Access to Information Act Number (TBS/SCT 350-62) Category Access to Information and Privacy Instructions Institutions must submit statistical reports annually to the Information and Privacy Policy Division on activities relating to the administration of the Access to Information Act that take place in their institutions. Reports submitted must be created in XML or XLS formats; however an HTML version is available here to illustrate the data elements that are required in the report. Statistical data for previous years are available in an open format.
