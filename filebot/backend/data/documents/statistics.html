Canadian Dental Care Plan statistics The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) is one of the country’s largest social programs and it is making the cost of dental care more affordable for millions of Canadian residents. Statistics for the CDCP are valid as of: February 28, 2026 and will be updated on a monthly basis.
