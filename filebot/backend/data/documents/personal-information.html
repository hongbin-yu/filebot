Change your personal information If you receive Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) benefits, you can now update your personal information directly in your My Service Canada Account (MSCA). Register or sign in to MSCA, go to the "Canadian Dental Care Plan" section on your dashboard and follow the instructions. The option to change your personal information for Employment Insurance (EI), Canada Pension Plan (CPP) or Old Age Security (OAS) benefits is currently not available in MSCA. To change your direct deposit information, mailing address or telephone number, you can: Request a call back Submit an eServiceCanada form to have a Service Canada representative call you back within 2 business days. Go in person Visit any Service Canada Office to get help in person. Find a Service Canada Office near you. You will need to show your ID and answer questions to verify your identity.
