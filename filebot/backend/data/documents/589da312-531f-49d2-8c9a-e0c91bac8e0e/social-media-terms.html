Social media terms of use The travel.gc.ca social media accounts are used as an extension of the material posted on Government of Canada websites. They are also meant to be platforms through which the Government of Canada can engage local audiences, answer relevant questions and reach Canadians abroad. In no way do we verify or confirm either the accuracy of user comments or their compliance with the guidelines outlined in these Terms of Use or those of each social media platform. These Terms of Use apply to the branded travel.gc.ca pages and content on Facebook and X (formerly Twitter). Availability We will update and monitor our social media accounts during local business hours (9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ET). Updates to the accounts may be subject to downtime that is out of travel.gc.ca’s control, as the servers for these channels are managed by a third party. As such, we accept no responsibility for the channels becoming unresponsive or unavailable. Content If you follow our accounts, you can expect information and tips on destination safety and security, local laws and customs, entry requirements, health conditions and emergency contact information for Canadian offices around the world, as well as: crisis evacuation information and mission closures travel advice and advisory updates seasonal updates (hurricanes, tropical storms, typhoons) travel-related public outreach events information about emergency assistance and other activities promotion of the Registration of Canadians Abroad service travel partner content from Global Affairs Canada (GAC), the Public Health Agency of Canada, the Canadian Border Services Agency, Citizenship and Immigration Canada, Passport Canada and the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority Post frequency The number of posts will vary depending on the number of travel advisories, travel warnings and other relevant material published each day. During some periods, we may post more or less frequently than average. You should expect to see new posts on travel.gc.ca’s accounts during local business hours (9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. ET) Monday to Friday. Any activity during holidays, after core hours and on weekends would likely be related to emergency or urgent communications of significant value to our social media community. Following Travel.gc.ca’s social media accounts are open to the public, so anyone with Internet access can view the content posted. Users can also “like” or follow travel.gc.ca’s channels, thereby allowing travel.gc.ca’s posts and updates to appear on their home feeds. We do not auto follow on X (formerly Twitter), or “friend” or “like” any individual or business that proactively “likes” our Facebook account. We follow only selected accounts from our partners, stakeholders and the public. Our following you or “liking” you on social media does not imply endorsement of any kind, and we reserve the right to stop following any individual or organization that posts inappropriate content. At this time, travel.gc.ca’s “Favourite Pages” include only other Government of Canada institutions and organizations that we believe are relevant to the mandate of our work. Favourite pages may include those of non-profit organizations, associations and other governmental agencies. The appearance of any particular page as a “favourite” of travel.gc.ca does not imply endorsement. We will take steps to remove or block users who do not abide by these Terms of Use. Users may also be reported to a given social media channel if they do not comply with the service provider’s Terms of Use. You must have a valid account on Facebook or X (formerly Twitter) to “like” or follow the travel.gc.ca accounts. Commenting policy Travel.gc.ca will not tolerate comments that are offensive or abusive to an individual or organization. We do not discriminate against any views but we reserve the right to edit, hide, or remove any comments that: are racist, hateful, sexist, homophobic, slanderous, insulting or life-threatening are serious, unproven, unsupported or inaccurate accusations against individuals or organizations are abusive, aggressive, coarse, explicit, vulgar, violent, obscene or pornographic are personal attacks and/or defamatory statements encourage or suggest illegal activity are not authored by the sender (or the copyright holder, if applicable) are solicitations, advertisements or endorsements of any financial, commercial or non-governmental agency.
