Canadian Dental Care Plan promotional toolkit: Overview Overview Social media Poster Banners and buttons Multilingual factsheets Testimonials The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) will help make the cost of oral health care more affordable for up to nine million uninsured Canadian residents with an adjusted family net income of less than $90,000. Applications for the CDCP are open in phases. We have prepared some tools and resources that stakeholders can use to help their patients, clients, and members better understand and access the plan.
