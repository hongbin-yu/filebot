Submit a declaration - individuals The nation-wide declaration period starts on January 19, 2026 and ends on March 31, 2026. To take part in the program you must create an account and declare your prohibited firearms during the declaration period. When the declaration period closes in March 2026, all submissions will be reviewed to determine which will move to the claim submission stage. Only those who have submitted a declaration within this timeframe will be considered eligible for the collection process and compensation. Please note that submitting a declaration does not guarantee you will receive compensation. Declarations will be reviewed primarily on a first come, first served basis and accepted based on availability of program funds. You are encouraged to submit your declaration as early as possible to increase the likelihood of receiving compensation. If you live in Saskatchewan or Alberta, please consult your provincial government for any additional laws or regulations that may affect your participation in the program. Collection appointments and options may vary by region and must comply with all applicable provincial laws. To receive a paper declaration to apply by mail, or if you would like to receive information in Indigenous languages (Mohawk, Anishinaabe (Ojibwe), Inuktut (South Baffin), Cree, Algonquin, Dene, or Mi'kmaq), please reach out to the program contact centre.
