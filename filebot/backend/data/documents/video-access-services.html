What is My Service Canada Account My Service Canada Account can help you access your government services and benefits. Watch our video to learn more! Transcript What is My Service Canada Account? It is a secure online portal that gives you access to government services and benefits. You can: view your Canadian Dental Care Plan information apply for Canada Pension Plan and Old Age Security benefits access Employment Insurance and pension information, such as: your payments your status updates, and your tax slips view your Social Insurance Number access your Canada Apprentice Loan online service account And much more! My Service Canada Account is convenient and safe. You can access your account anytime, from any device. Go to canada.ca/MyServiceCanadaAccount to register for your account! Page details 2024-10-29
